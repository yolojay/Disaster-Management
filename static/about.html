About the Project

An AI-powered multi-agent disaster management system protecting coastal communities from cyclone threats using IBM watsonx.ai and Granite-4.

🔷 IBM watsonx.ai 🧠 IBM Granite-4 🤖 Multi-Agent AI 🏆 Hackathon 2026
Context
The Challenge & Our Response
🌊
The Problem

Coastal communities face fragmented disaster response — delayed cyclone information, poorly coordinated evacuations, resource misallocation, and slow post-disaster assessments. Traditional systems cannot keep pace with fast-developing cyclones and cannot connect all emergency activities into a unified response.

Each agency operates in isolation: meteorology, fisheries, civil administration, and relief bodies rarely share a single picture of the evolving threat. The result is lost time, duplication of effort, and preventable loss of life.

🧠
Our Solution

A multi-agent AI platform coordinating five specialist agents simultaneously — cyclone prediction, fishermen safety, evacuation planning, resource coordination, and damage assessment — all powered by IBM Granite and orchestrated into a single unified executive briefing.

Decision-makers receive one coherent, actionable document in under 60 seconds instead of waiting hours for inter-agency reports. The system runs on IBM watsonx.ai with full IAM authentication and a production-ready FastAPI backend.
